## Aim

To explore prompting techniques for video generation using an AI video-generation tool by creating a detailed prompt and generating a realistic video.

## Objective

- To understand AI-based video generation.
- To create a basic prompt for video generation.
- To refine the prompt with details about the subject, movement, lighting, and background.
- To generate a video using Gemini.
- To observe the effect of detailed prompting on the generated video.

## Tool Used

**Google Gemini – AI Video Generation**

## Procedure

1. A video concept was selected without using a reference video.
2. A basic description of the required scene was prepared.
3. The prompt was refined by adding details about the subject, background, lighting, camera movement, and visual style.
4. The final prompt was entered into Google Gemini.
5. Gemini generated a video based on the given prompt.
6. The generated video was observed for visual quality, movement, lighting, and consistency.
7. The final generated video was saved and uploaded as the experiment output.

## Prompt Used

```text
Create a realistic cinematic video of a young man walking slowly along a beautiful beach during sunset. The ocean waves gently move toward the shore, and the golden sunlight reflects on the water. The man is wearing a simple black shirt and jeans and is walking naturally while looking toward the ocean. Show palm trees moving slightly in the sea breeze in the background. Use smooth cinematic camera movement, starting with a wide shot and slowly moving closer to the subject. Include realistic body movement, natural shadows, detailed textures, warm sunset lighting, realistic ocean waves, and a peaceful atmosphere. Maintain consistent appearance and proportions throughout the video. Create smooth, natural motion with high-quality realistic visuals and cinematic composition.
